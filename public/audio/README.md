# Sounds for the activities

Both files were generated by `scripts/make-audio.mjs` from plain sine waves. They are
original, made for this project, and carry no third-party rights (MIT, like the rest of the
repository).

| File | What it is | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| `warm-pad.wav` | A soft, warm chord that breathes slowly, 60 s | One song, one first step |
| `low-drone.wav` | A calm low drone with a slow swell, 60 s | Wind down the evening |

Mono, 16-bit PCM WAV at 11 025 Hz, about 1.3 MB each, with a 4 s fade in and a 6 s fade out.

To regenerate: `node scripts/make-audio.mjs`.
To use other recordings: put the files here and change the `src` line for each sound in
`lib/activities/sounds.ts`. That is the only place the paths are written.
